# Changelog

- **Automatic metadata:** embedded title, artist, and artwork import in the background, alongside duration scanning. Your manual edits are preserved. Refresh a stack to reread its tags and duration.
- **Playback source:** the player shows the originating stack or playlist and its artwork. All tracks, Favorites, and Recently played use embedded artwork when available.
- **A queue you can arrange:** drag to reorder, move or remove entries, and clear upcoming tracks. Play or Next starts an idle queue.
- **Shared color choices:** accents, stacks, and playlists use the same preset palette, including white. Title and artist sorting support both directions.
- **Interface polish:** wider views, consistent icons, cleaner hover areas and alignment, and scan notifications that stay evenly spaced.

## Downloads

- **macOS (Apple Silicon):** `Mimo-0.4.7.dmg`. Existing supported macOS installs can also use Check for updates once the release is published.
- **Windows (x64):** `Mimo_0.4.7_x64-setup.exe`. This is a separate installer; Windows automatic update delivery is not included in this release.

Mimo is an alpha app. macOS builds use ad-hoc signing, and the Windows installer is unsigned, so your operating system may ask you to approve the app. Your library stays local, and your audio plays directly from your linked folders.
